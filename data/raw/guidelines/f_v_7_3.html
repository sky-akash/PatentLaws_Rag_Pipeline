7.3 International preliminary examination report (IPER) 

For international applications entering the European phase with an international preliminary examination report, the examining division should carefully consider the position taken in that IPER before departing from it. This may be necessary where the claims have been changed, the applicant successfully refutes the objection (either of which may happen in response to the IPER, see A‑XIV, 6.3E‑IX, 3.3.1) or the rules regarding unity of invention were misinterpreted; see also F‑V, 7.1 and F-V, 7.2 above.
