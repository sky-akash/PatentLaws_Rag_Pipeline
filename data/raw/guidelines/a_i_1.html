1. Overview 

Part A of the Guidelines deals with:

(i)the filing of European patent applications ("Euro-direct applications") and the requirements and procedures to be followed in the various aspects of the formalities examination of such of European patent applications (chapters see A‑II to A-VI)

(ii)formalities matters of a more general nature that can arise during the application procedure or post-grant stage (chapters see A‑VII and A-VIII)

(iii)the presentation and execution of drawings and figurative representations accompanying a European patent application Euro-direct application (chapter see A‑IX)

(iv)fee matters (chapter see A‑X)

(v)inspection of files, communication of information contained in files, consultation of the European Patent Register and issue of certified copies (chapter see A‑XI)

(vi)the requirements and procedures to be followed in the proceedings before the EPO as designated/elected Office on and after entry into the European phase ("Euro-PCT applications") (see A‑XII to A‑XV).
