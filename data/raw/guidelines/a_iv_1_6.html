1.6 Authorisations 

The provisions of A‑VIII, 1.5A‑VIII, 1.7 and A-VIII, 1.6A‑VIII, 1.8 apply with regard to authorisations in respect of the divisional application. If, according to these provisions, the representative has to file an authorisation, they may act on the basis of an individual authorisation filed in respect of the parent application only if it expressly empowers them to file divisional applications.
