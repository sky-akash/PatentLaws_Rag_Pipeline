1.3.1 Communication in the event of deficiencies as described in D‑IV, 1.2.1 which, if not remedied, will lead to the opposition being deemed not filed

The communication will indicate the deficiencies noted in accordance with D‑IV, 1.2.1 and will state that the opposition will be deemed not filed unless the deficiency or deficiencies are remedied within the time limits indicated in D‑IV, 1.2.1.
