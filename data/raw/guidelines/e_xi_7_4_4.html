7.4.4 Response to communication under Rule 58 filed with the appeal

If, in response to the Receiving Section's refusal of the application under Art. 90(5), the related deficiencies are fully rectified so as to overcome the grounds for refusal, interlocutory revision is granted by the Receiving Section.

Example

On the date of filing, the drawings did not comply with the requirements set by the President under Rule 49(2). The application was subsequently refused (Art. 90(5)) because the applicant had filed the same poor-quality drawings in reply to the communication under Rule 58. When filing an appeal complying with the requirements of Art. 108, the applicant also files drawings of sufficient quality and so corrects the deficiency on which the refusal was based. Since the ground for the refusal has been overcome and the reasoning in the decision under appeal no longer applies, the Receiving Section grants interlocutory revision and does not refer the case to the boards of appeal.
