6.2 Cancellation of the registration 

A registration of licences or other rights is cancelled on request, supported by documents showing that the right has lapsed or by the written consent of the proprietor of the right to the cancellation. Rule 22(2) applies mutatis mutandis, i.e. the cancellation may be subject to the payment of an administrative fee. Cancellation is only possible until publication of the mention of the grant.
