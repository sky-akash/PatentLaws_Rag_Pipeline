1.5 Debit orders for deposit accounts held with the EPO 

For European patent applications, debit orders for the fees due must be filed in an accepted electronic format (see A‑X, 4.2.3), irrespective of how the application itself is filed. If an application is filed with a competent national authority (Art. 75(1)(b)) on paper, a paper debit order on mandatory Form 1020 for the fees intended to be paid is accepted by way of exception if it is included with that application on filing (see the Arrangements for deposit accounts (ADA), Supplementary publication 2, OJ EPO 2024, and the decision of the President of the EPO dated 25 September 2024, OJ EPO 2024, A8112 February 2025, OJ EPO 2025, A17). Paper Form 1020 is not accepted if filed direct with the EPO.
