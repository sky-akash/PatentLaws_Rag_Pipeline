3.1 Basic principle 

Having different patent proprietors for different designated contracting states will not affect the unity of the European patent in opposition proceedings, since such persons are to be regarded as joint proprietors (see D‑I, 6, second and third paragraphs).

In particular, the text of the European patent will be uniform for all designated contracting states unless otherwise provided for in the EPC (see D‑VII, 3.2 and H‑III, 4).
