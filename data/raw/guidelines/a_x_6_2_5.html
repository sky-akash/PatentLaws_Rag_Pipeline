6.2.5 Noting of loss of rights 

If applicants who have been sent a communication under Rule 112(1) noting non-compliance with a payment time limit claim that the payment was made in due time under Art. 7(1) RFees, Art. 7(3) RFees and Art. 7(4) RFees or in accordance with the safety provision for replenishment of deposit accounts, they must apply for a decision under Rule 112(2) and submit the requisite evidence. As an auxiliary request, applicants are advised to request further processing.
