4.1 General remarks 

The request for grant must be made on the appropriate EPO form (EPO Form 1001), even though the request (the indication that a patent is sought, referred to in A‑II, 4.1(a)) need initially be in no particular form. The latest version of EPO Form 1001 can be accessed in EPO Online Filing and Online Filing 2.0 or downloaded in PDF format from the EPO website (epo.org) (see A‑II, 1.1.1).

For filing with EPO Online Filing, applicants should always use the latest version of the software (see A‑II, 1.1.1(i)) and, fFor paper filings and filings made with the EPO Contingency Upload Service, applicants should always use the latest version of the form.
