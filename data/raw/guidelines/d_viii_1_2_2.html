1.2.2 Revocation for failure to file a translation or to file a formally compliant version of amended text passages 

Under Rule 82(2) in conjunction with Rule 82(3), if the patent proprietor fails in due time to:

(i)file a translation of the amended claims in the two official languages of the EPO other than the language of the proceedings (see D‑VI, 7.2.3) or

(ii)file a formally compliant verbatim version of the amended text passages (see E‑III, 8.7.3),

the European patent will be revoked.
