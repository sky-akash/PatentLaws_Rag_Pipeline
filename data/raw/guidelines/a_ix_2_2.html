2.2 Reproducibility of drawings 

The drawings must be presented in such a way as to allow their electronic or direct reproduction, in particular by scanning, and photography, electrostatic processes, photo offset and microfilming in an unlimited number of copies. When filed by means of electronic communication, drawings must also be sufficiently rich in contrast and suitable to be clearly displayed at a resolution of 300 dpi.

Rule 49(2)OJ EPO 2025, A49
