5.4 Establishment and issue of the technical opinion 

Once any deficiencies referred to in E‑XII, 5.1 E‑XIII, 5.1 or E‑XII, 5.2 E-XIII, 5.2, above have been remedied, the examining division draws up the technical opinion as soon as possible.

The opinion is sent to the national court. Any papers received from the court which belong to the national proceedings are sent back with the opinion.
