6.8.4 Translation of previous application already filed 

Where the applicant has already supplied a translation of the previous application in connection with a request to base late-filed parts of the description or drawings on the claimed priority under Rule 56 (see A‑II, 5.4(vi)) or to base correct application documents or parts on the claimed priority under Rule 56a (see A‑II, 6.4(vi)), the applicant does not need to file it again.
