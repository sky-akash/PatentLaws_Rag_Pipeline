3.1.1 Time limits covered 

Failure to observe the time limit must have the direct consequence of causing the refusal of the European patent application or of a request, the deemed withdrawal of the European patent application, the revocation of the European patent or the loss of any other right or means of redress. This means, for example, that in opposition proceedings there can be no re-establishment of rights in respect of the time limits for the patent proprietor's submission of observations on the written statements of the other parties to the proceedings or on communications from the opposition division. Likewise, there can be no re-establishment of rights where the time limit for paying the renewal fees under Rule 51(1) is missed, as valid payment is still possible under Rule 51(2).

Art. 122(1)

Re-establishment of rights is ruled out in respect of all periods for which further processing is available and in respect of the period for requesting re-establishment of rights. This means that re-establishment of rights comes into play where further processing is excluded in respect of a specific period or where the time limit for requesting further processing has expired. In the latter case, re-establishment of rights is to be requested in respect of the time limit for requesting further processing (see E‑VIII, 2), and not in respect of the originally missed time limit.

Art. 122(4)
Rule 136(3)

A "time limit" is taken to mean a specific period of time within which an act to be performed in proceedings before the EPO must be completed (see E‑VIII, 1.1). Re-establishment of rights is therefore not admissible e.g. in respect of failure to attend on the date of oral proceedings.

Examples of time limits in respect of which re-establishment of rights may be requested are the ones for:

Rule 131(1)

– paying a renewal fee plus additional fee 

Rule 51(2)

– requesting further processing in respect of the time limit for replying to a communication from the examining division under Art. 94(3)

Rule 135(1)

– filing the translation of any amended claims in opposition proceedings 

Rule 82(2)

– filing the request for a decision by the opposition division on the awarding of costs 

Rule 88(3)

– filing notice of appeal and/or grounds of appeal 

Art. 108

– filing a petition for review by the Enlarged Board of Appeal. 

Art. 112a(4)
