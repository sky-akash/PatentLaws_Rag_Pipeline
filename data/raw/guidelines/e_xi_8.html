8. Rules of Procedure of the Boards of Appeal 

Details of the procedure before the boards of appeal, including the acceleration of appeal proceedings, can be found in the Rules of Procedure of the Boards of Appeal (see OJ EPO 2019, A63, as amended by OJ EPO 2021, A19). The Enlarged Board of Appeal has also adopted Rules of Procedure (see OJ EPO 2015, A35).
