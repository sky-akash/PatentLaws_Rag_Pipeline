3.2 Legal consequence if the translation is not filed

The legal consequences of non-compliance vary depending on which documents were not filed in due time:

If the applicant does not furnish the translation of any of items A‑XIII, 3.1(i) and A‑XIII, 3.1(ii) in A‑XIII, 3.1 within the 31-month period, the application is deemed to be withdrawn under Rule 160(1).
