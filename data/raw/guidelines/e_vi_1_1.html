1.1 General remarks 

In proceedings before it, the EPO examines the facts of its own motion; it is not restricted in this examination to the facts, evidence and arguments provided by the parties and the relief sought. The competent department must comply with this principle of examination by the EPO of its own motion during all proceedings pending before it. Thus, once proceedings have been initiated, e.g. once a valid request for examination or an admissible notice of opposition has been filed (although it may subsequently be withdrawn), if there is reason to believe, e.g. from personal knowledge or from observations presented by third parties, that there are facts and evidence not yet considered which completely or partly prejudice the granting or maintenance of the European patent, the EPO must include these facts and evidence in those it examines of its own motion under Art. 114(1). See D‑V, 2 for the extent of substantive examination of the facts and evidence in opposition proceedings.
