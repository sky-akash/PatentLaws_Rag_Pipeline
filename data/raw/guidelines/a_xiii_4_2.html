4.2 Legal consequence and remedy if the filing fee, including the additional fee, is not paid

If the filing fee, including any page fee due, is not paid in due time, the Euro-PCT application will be deemed to be withdrawn and the applicant will be informed of the loss of rights (Rule 160(2)). The communication under Rule 160(2) and the communication under Rule 112(1) are sent together in the same communication.

Rule 160

The loss of rights may be remedied by a request for further processing under Art. 121 (E‑VIII, 2). The further processing fee is calculated on the basis of the number of pages on file at the relevant period's expiry and for which the additional fee, calculated as set out in A‑XIII, 4.1.2, has not been paid. If there was an error only in paying the correct page fee and the basic fee was duly paid, that basic fee is not relevant for establishing the fee for further processing.

Art. 121

If the time limit for requesting further processing has been missed, a request for re-establishment of rights under Art. 122 in respect of the period under Rule 135(1) may be filed (E‑VIII, 3).

Art. 122

Furthermore, if the filing fee is not paid in full, including any page fee due, the international application will not be considered comprised in the state of the art under Art. 54(3) pursuant to Art. 153(5) and Rule 165 (A‑XIII, 10.2;
G‑IV, 5.2).
