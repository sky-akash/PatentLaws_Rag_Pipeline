9.2 Fee-related support measures for small entities and micro-entities  

Applicants fulfilling certain criteria are eligible for fee reductions under the language arrangements (see A‑X, 9.3) and a fee reduction scheme specifically for micro-entities (see A‑X, 9.4). If applicants are eligible for a reduction under both schemes, the fee in question is reduced twiceboth reductions are applied sequentially (see the decision of the Administrative Council of 14 December 2023, OJ EPO 2024, A3, and the notice from the EPO dated 25 January 2024, OJ EPO 2024, A8, which includes examples).
