13.3 Additional fee for divisional applications 

Regarding the additional fee payable as part of the filing fee for divisional applications of second or subsequent generations filed on or after 1 April 2014, see A‑IV, 1.4.1.1 and the notice from the EPO dated 8 January 2014, OJ EPO 2014, A22.

For fee reductions under the language arrangements, see A‑X, 9.3.1 and A-X, 9.3.2; for fee reductions under the scheme for micro-entities, see A‑X, 9.4.1 and A-X, 9.4.2.
