7. Changes in claim category in opposition 

An amendment can be in the form of a change in the category of a claim, possibly combined with a change in the technical features of the invention. Firstly it must be clear that this amendment is necessitated by grounds for opposition (see H‑II, 3.1). If that is not the case, a change of category is refused.

Even if this condition is fulfilled, the opposition division exercises great caution in allowing a change of claim category, since the protection as conferred by the claims may thus be extended (Art. 123(3)).
