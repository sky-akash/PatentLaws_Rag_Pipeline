5.1 Verification of claimed priority dates 

Where the validity of a priority claim cannot be verified at the search stage (see B‑XI, 4), the date of filing of the European patent application accorded by the Receiving Section must be taken as the relevant date for the search. (However, see B‑VI, 3 on the relevant date for the search for conflicting applications.)
