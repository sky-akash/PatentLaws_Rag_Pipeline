11.2.1 Tables in the description 

For the sake of convenience, the tables may also be grouped together on one or more sheets pages annexed to the description and paginated with it.

If two or more tables are necessary, each should be identified by a Roman number, independently of the pagination of the description or drawings or of the figure numbering, or by a capital letter, or by a title indicating its contents, or by some other means.

Each line or column in a table must begin with an entry explaining what it represents and, if necessary, the units used.

Both characters and tables alike must satisfy the requirements of Art. 2(7) and (4) of the decision of the President of the EPO dated 25 November 20227 July 2025 (OJ EPO 2025, A49) regarding the maximum usable surface areas of sheetspages.
