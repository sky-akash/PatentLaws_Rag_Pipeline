11.2.2 Payment of designation fee 

All states party to the EPC at the time of filing a European patent application are automatically designated when the application is filed. The designation fee, however, may be paid later (see A‑III, 11.2.1).

Rule 39(1)

Payment of the designation fee covers all contracting states, except those for which the designation has been expressly withdrawn.
