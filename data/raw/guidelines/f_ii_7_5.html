7.5 Omission of matter from publication 

Generally, the Receiving Section will deal with matter falling under category 1(a) and may have dealt with matter obviously falling within category 1(b). Any such matter not omitted from the application as published must be removed, together with any other prohibited matter, during the application's examination. The applicant is informed of the category under which matter is required to be removed.
