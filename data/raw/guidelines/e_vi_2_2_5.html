2.2.5 Costs 

In opposition, relevant facts and evidence submitted at a late stage of the proceedings ‒ e.g. not until the oral proceedings ‒ could lead to a decision on apportionment of costs (see D‑IX, 1.2) if this is requested. As regards the costs which a party may be ordered to bear as a result of late submissions, see also D‑IX, 1.4.
