4. International exhibition 

In case G‑V, 1(ii), the application must be filed within six months of the invention's disclosure at the exhibition to ensure that the display does not prejudice the application. Furthermore, the applicant must state, when filing the application, that the invention has been so displayed, and must also file a supporting certificate within four months, giving the particulars required by Rule 25 (see A‑IV, 3). The exhibitions recognised are published in the Official Journalon the EPO website (epo.org).
