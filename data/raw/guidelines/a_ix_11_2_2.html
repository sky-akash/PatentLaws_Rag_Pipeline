11.2.2 Tables in the claims 

The claims may include tables if their subject-matter indicates a need for them. In this case, the tables must be included in the text of the relevant claim; they may not be annexed to the claims nor may reference be made to tables contained in or annexed to the description. The claims may refer to other application documents only where this is absolutely necessary (see F‑IV, 4.17). The mere desire to avoid copying said documents does not constitute absolute necessity.
