Rule 119
Examination of evidence before the European Patent Office

(1) The Examining Division, Opposition Division or Board of Appeal may commission one of its members to examine the evidence adduced. 

(2) Before a party, witness or expert parties, witnesses or experts may be heard, he they shall be informed that the European Patent Office may request the competent court in the their country of residence of the person concerned to re-examine his their testimony under oath or in an equally binding form.

(3) The parties may attend an investigation and may put relevant questions to the testifying party, witness or expert.
