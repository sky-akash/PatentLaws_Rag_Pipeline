Rule 154199
Amendment of the list of professional representatives

(1)200 The entry of a professional representative shall be deleted from the list of professional representatives if he they so requests request or if, despite a reminder, he fails they fail to pay the applicable annual subscription to the Institute within five months from either:

(a) 1 January for members on the list at that date; or 

(b) the date of entry for members entered on the list after 1 January of the year for which the subscription is due. 

(2) Without prejudice to any disciplinary measures taken under Article 134a, paragraph 1(c), the entry of a professional representative may be deleted ex officio only:

(a) in the event of his their death or legal incapacity;

(b) where he is they are no longer a national of one of the Contracting States, unless he was they were granted an exemption under Article 134, paragraph 7(a);

(c) where he they no longer has his have their place of business or employment within one of the Contracting States.

(3)201 Any person entered on the list of professional representatives under Article 134, paragraphs (2) or Article 134, paragraphs (3), whose entry has been deleted shall, upon request, be re-entered on that list if the conditions for deletion no longer exist.
